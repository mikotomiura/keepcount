In spring the field is still wet from the snow. Small green points come up between the stones, and the wind is cold in the morning and soft by noon.

Summer is loud. The cicadas start before the light, the road shimmers, and the river is low enough to cross on foot. Evenings last a long time.

In autumn the air turns dry and clear. Leaves go yellow from the top of the tree down, and the first cold night comes without warning. We bring the chairs inside.

Winter is quiet. The ground is hard, the sky is white, and sound carries far. Then one day the light stays a little longer, and the year starts again.
