The rule takes two numbers and gives three. The first number sets how fine the pattern is: more waves, closer together. The second sets how deep it is: higher hills and lower valleys. Both together set how sharp the edges are.

The rule does not say where the numbers come from. Here they come from typing: how quickly the keys follow one another, and how long the hands rest. Nothing about the words themselves goes in.

A seed draws the phases of the waves. The same numbers with another seed give another surface of the same kind. So a tile is not a picture of the typing. It is one surface the rule allows for that typing.
