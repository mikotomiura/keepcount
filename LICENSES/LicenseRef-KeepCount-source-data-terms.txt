Terms of the keystroke data counted by runs v6 to v6s of KeepCount

The results of runs v6 to v6j (results/prototype_v6*/) and the values, tables and lists of
article/jowr/values/ (*.json and *.tex) count keystroke data of others. They keep the terms of
their sources:

- Counts and values derived from KUPA-KEYS (Velentzas et al., LREC-COLING 2024,
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/ALTACambridge/KUPA-KEYS) are under the Creative Commons
  Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode
- Counts and values derived from 136M Keystrokes (Dhakal, Feit, Kristensson and Oulasvirta,
  CHI 2018, https://doi.org/10.1145/3173574.3174220) are for non-commercial use with
  attribution to its authors.
- Counts and values derived from the essays of the Kaggle competition Linking Writing Processes
  to Writing Quality are under the competition's rules.

The keystroke data themselves are not redistributed in this archive. Where a file draws on more
than one source, the terms of every source it draws on apply.
